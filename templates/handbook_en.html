ℹ Quick Guide for Testers:
  • Orange Button: VOX has been activated (device is transmitting automatically). It may happen that the PTT briefly lights up orange after being released, even if VOX is not active. Good grief, I\'m working on it.
  • S-SCAN: Scan stops when a signal is detected. Each press of the button ends the scan. The scan speed can be adjusted in milliseconds below.
  • Super Sync: Hold for 3 seconds to force channel 01 (if the display is asynchronous). It\'s possible that the modulation button will operate asynchronously. This is obviously undesirable when using a remote. Solution: Open ASQ and press the Mode button in the web interface until you hear the typical FM noise, regardless of what the web interface displays. Then use the SYNC button.
  • Numeric Keypad: Channel selection via keypad: Always enter two digits (e.g., 07).
  • SKIP: The "Skip PA / CW" function applies to those who have already installed the new firmware and deactivated PA in the Mode Switch menu.
  • SCAN SPEED: S-Scan is software-controlled and independent of the AE5900\'s integrated scan function. The speed can be specified in milliseconds.
  • P - Label: The P1-P4 buttons can be visually labeled here. According to what is set on the AE5900 under the Mic Type 2 menu item.
  • Vol - and Vol + : The Mic Gain of the built-in sound card / Mumble.
  • PTT Timeout: The PTT Timeout is for added security. It prevents the AE5900 from continuing to transmit for an extended period after a connection loss.
  • MW: Multiwatch scans predefined channels. Simply enter the channels to be scanned, separated by commas. Example: 01,04,09,28
  • MUTE: This mutes the AE5900.
  • A/SQ + / -: Here, the ASQ level (if enabled) or the squelch is set. ASQ has 9 levels, Squelch has 80 levels.
  • SET A/SQ 1/0: To avoid unnecessary button presses: A reset that returns the device from a maximum of 80 set levels to 1 (ASQ) or 0 (Squelch).
  • CLAR / STEP: The clarifier is only enabled in CW, USB, and LSB modes. Press STEP to select between 1, 10, or 100 Hz, and then press CLAR + or CLAR -.
  • LOCK: Locks all buttons on the AE5900 and in the WebUI, except for the Lock button.
